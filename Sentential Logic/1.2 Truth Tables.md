# Truth Tables
When we evaluate the truth or falsity of a statement, we assign to it one of the labels **true** or **false**, and this label is called its **truth value**.


| $P$ | $Q$ | $P\land Q$ |
| --- | --- | ---------- |
| F   | F   | F          |
| F   | T   | F          |
| T   | F   | F          |
| T   | T   | T          |

| $P$ | $\neg P$ |
| --- | -------- |
| F   | T        |
| T   | F        |

How to represent an argument symbolically?

$$
\frac{\begin{align}
& P\lor Q \\
& \neg P \\

\end{align}}{
\therefore Q
}
$$

**De Morgan's laws**
$\neg(P\land Q)$ is equivalent to $\neg P\lor \neg Q$
$\neg(P\lor Q)$ is equivalent to $\neg P\land \neg Q$

**Commutative laws**
$P\land Q$ is equivalent to $Q\land P$
$P\lor Q$ is equivalent to $Q\lor P$

**Associative laws**
$P\land(Q\land R)$ is equivalent to $(P\land Q)\land R$
$P\lor(Q\lor R)$ is equivalent to $(P\lor Q)\lor R$

**Idempotent laws**
$P\land P$ is equivalent to $P$
$P\lor P$ is equivalent to $P$

**Distributive laws**
$P\land(Q\lor R)$ is equivalent to $(P\land Q)\lor(P\land R)$
$P\lor(Q\land R)$ is equivalent to $(P\lor Q)\land(P\lor R)$

**Absorption laws**
$P\lor(P\land Q)$ is equivalent to $P$
$P\land(P\lor Q)$ is equivalent to $P$

**Double Negation law**
$\neg \neg P$ is equivalent to P

Formulas that are always true, such as $P\lor \neg P$, are called **tautologies**. Similarly, formulas that are always false are called **contradiction**.

**Tautology laws**
$P\land$(a tautology) is equivalent to $P$.
$P\lor$(a tautology) is a tautology.
$\neg$(a tautology) is a tautology.

**Contradiction laws**
$P\land$(a contradiction) is a contradition.
$P\lor$(a contradiction) is equivalent to $P$.
$\neg$(a contradiction) is equivalent to tautology.
