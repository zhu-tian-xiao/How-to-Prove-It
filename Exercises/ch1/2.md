2.Analyse the logical forms of the following statements:
(a) Either John and Bill are both telling the truth, or neither of them is.
(b) I'll have either fish or chicken, but I won't have both fish and mashed potatoes.
(c) $3$ is common divisor of $6, 9$ and  $15$.

(a) $(J\land B)\lor(\neg J\land \neg B)$, where $J$ stands for "John is telling the truth," $B$ stands for "Bill is telling the truth."
(b) $F$ stands for "I'll have fish," $C$ stands for "I'll have chicken," $P$ stands for "I have mashed potatoes."
(c) $R$ stands for "$3$ is a common divisor of $6$", $S$ stands for "$3$ is a common divisor of $9$", $T$ stands for "$3$ is a common divisor of $12$", $R\land S\land T$
