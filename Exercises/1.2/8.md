Use truth tables to determine which of the following formulas are equivalent to each other:

(a) $(P\land Q)\lor(\neg P\land \neg Q)$

(b) $\neg P\lor Q$

(c) $(P\lor \neg Q)\land(Q\lor \neg P)$

(d) $\neg(P\lor Q)$

(e) $(Q\land P)\lor \neg P$

$(P\land Q)\lor(\neg P\land \neg Q)$ is equivalent to $[(P\land Q)\lor \neg P]\land[(P\land Q)\lor \neg Q]$, which is equivalent to $(Q\lor \neg P)\land(P\lor\neg Q)$, so (a) is equivalent to (c).

$(Q\land P)\lor \neg P$ is equivalent to $Q\lor \neg P$, so (b) is equivalent to (e).
