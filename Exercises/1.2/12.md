Use the laws stated in the text to find simpler formulas equivalent to these formulas. 

(a) $\neg(\neg P\lor Q)\lor(P\land \neg R)$.

(b) $\neg(\neg P\land Q)\lor(P\land \neg R)$.

(c) $(P\land R)\lor[\neg R\land(P\lor Q)]$.

---
(a) is equivalent to $(P\land \neg Q)\lor(P\land \neg R)$, which is equivalent to $P\land(\neg Q\lor \neg R)$.

(b) is equivalent to $(P\lor \neg Q)\lor(P\land \neg R)$, which is equivalent to $P\lor \neg Q$.

(c) is equivalent to $[(P\land R)\lor \neg R]\land[(P\land R)\lor(P\lor Q)]$, which is equivalent to $(P\lor \neg R)\land(P\lor Q)$, which is equivalent to $P\lor(\neg R\land Q)$.
