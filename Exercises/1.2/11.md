Use the laws stated in the text to find simpler formulas equivalent to these formulas. 

(a) $\neg(\neg P\land\neg Q)$.

(b) $(P\land Q)\lor(P\land \neg Q)$.

(c) $\neg(P\land \neg Q)\lor(\neg P\land Q)$.

---
(a) is equivalent to $P\lor Q$.

(b) is equivalent to $P\land(Q\lor \neg Q)$ [[Distributive laws]], which is equivalent to $P$.

(c) is equivalent to $(\neg P\lor Q)\lor(\neg P\land Q)$, which is equivalent to $Q\lor(\neg P\lor(\neg P\land Q))$,  which is equivalent to $\neg P\lor Q$ [[Absorption laws]].
