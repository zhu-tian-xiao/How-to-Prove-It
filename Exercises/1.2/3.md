In this exercise we will use the symbol $+$ to mean exclusive or. In other words, $P+Q$ means "$P$ or $Q$, but not both."

(a) Make a truth table for $P+Q$

(b) Find a formula using only the connectives $\land$, $\lor$, and $\neg$ that equivalent to $P+Q$. Justify your answer with a truth table.

| $P$ | $Q$ | $P+Q$ |
| --- | --- | ----- |
| F   | F   | F     |
| F   | T   | T     |
| T   | F   | T     |
| T   | T   | F     |

(b) $(P\lor Q)\land(\neg P\lor \neg Q)$

|  T  |  F  |     T     |          T          |                  T                  |
