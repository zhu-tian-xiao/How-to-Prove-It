Let P stand for the statement “I will buy the pants” and S for the statement “I will buy the shirt.” What English sentences are represented by the following formulas?

(a) $\neg(P\land \neg S)$.

(b) $\neg P\land \neg S$.

(c) $\neg P\lor \neg S$.

---
(a) I won’t buy the pants without the shirt.

(b) I won’t buy the pants and I won’t buy the shirt.

(c) Either I won’t buy the pants or I won’t buy the shirt.
