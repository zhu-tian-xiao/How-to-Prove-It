Analyse the logical forms of the following statements:

(a) Alice and Bob are not both in the room.

(b) Alice and Bob are both not in the room.

(c) Either Alice or Bob is not in the room.

(d) Neither Alice nor Bob is in the room.

---
Let $A$ stands for "Alice is in the room," $B$ stands for "Bob is in the room."

(a) $\neg(A\land B)$.
(b) $\neg A \land \neg B$.
(c) $\neg A \lor \neg B$.
(d) $\neg A \land \neg B$.

(a) is equivalent to (c).

(b) is equivalent to (d).
