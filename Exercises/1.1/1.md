Analyse the logical forms of the following statements:

(a) We'll have either a reading assignment or homework problems, but we won't have both homework problems and a test.

(b) You won't go skiing, or you will and there won't be any snow.

(c) $\sqrt{ 7 }\nleq 2$.

---
(a) $(R\lor H)\land \neg(H\land T)$, where $R$ stands for the statement "We'll have a reading assignment," $H$ stands for "We'll have homework problems," and $T$ stands for "We'll have a test."

(b) $\neg G \lor(G\land \neg S)$, where $G$ stands for "You'll go skiing," and $S$ stands for "There will be snow."

(c) $\neg[(\sqrt{ 7 }<2)\lor(\sqrt{ 7 }=2)]$.
