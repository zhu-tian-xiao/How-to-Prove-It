Let S stand for the statement “Steve is happy” and G for “George is happy.” What English sentences are represented by the following formulas?

(a) $(S\lor G)\land(\neg S\lor \neg G)$

(b) $[S\lor(G\land \neg S)]\lor \neg G$

(c) $S\lor[G\land(\neg S\lor \neg G)]$

---
(a) Exactly one of Steve and George is happy.

(b) Either Steve is happy, or George is happy and Steve is not happy, or George is not happy. / This is a tautology.

(c) Steve is happy, or George is happy.
