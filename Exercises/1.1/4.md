Analyse the logical forms of the following statements:

(a) Either both Ralph and Ed are tall, or both of them are handsome.

(b) Both Ralph and Ed are either tall or handsome.

(c) Both Ralph and Ed are neither tall nor handsome.

(d) Neither Ralph nor Ed is both tall and handsome.

---
Let $R_{t}$ stands for "Ralph is tall, "$E_{t}$ stands for "Ed is tall," $R_{h}$ stands for "Ralph is handsome," $E_{h}$ stands for "Ed is handsome."

(a) $(R_{t}\land E_{t})\lor(R_{h}\land E_{h})$.

(b) $(R_{t}\lor R_{h})\land(E_{t}\lor E_{h})$.

(c) $(\neg R_{t}\land \neg R_{h})\land(\neg E_{t}\land \neg E_{h})$.

(d)$\neg(R_{t}\land R_{h})\land \neg(E_{t}\land E_{h})$.
