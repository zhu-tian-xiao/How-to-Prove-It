It is shown in this section that for any sets $A$ and $B$, $(A\cup B)\setminus B \subseteq A$.

(a) Give an example of two sets $A$ and $B$ for which $(A\cup B)\setminus B\neq A$.

(b) Show that for all sets $A$ and $B$, $(A\cup B)\setminus B=A\setminus B$.

---
(a) $A=\{ 1,2 \}$, $B=\{ 2,3 \}$, $A\cup B=\{1,2,3\}$, $(A\cup B)\setminus B=\{ 1 \}\neq A$.

(b) $x \in (A\cup B)\setminus B$ is equivalent to $(x \in A \lor x \in B)\land x\not\in B$,
which is equivalent to $x \in A\setminus B$.
