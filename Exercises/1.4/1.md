Let $A=\{ 1,3,12,35 \}$, $B=\{ 3,7,12,20 \}$, and $C=\{ x\mid x \text{ is a prime number} \}$. List the elements of the following set. Are any of the sets below disjoint from any of the others? Are any of the sets below subsets of any others?

(a) $A\cap B$.

(b) $(A\cup B)\setminus C$.

(c) $A\cup(B\setminus C)$.

(a) $\{ 3 ,12\}$.

(b) $\{ 1,12,20,35 \}$.

(c) $\{ 1,3,12,20,35 \}$.

(a) and (b) are subsets of (c).
