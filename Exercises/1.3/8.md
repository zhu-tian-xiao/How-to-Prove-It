What are the truth sets of the following statements? List a few elements of the truth set if you can.

(a) Elizabeth Taylor was once married to $x$.

(b) $x$ is a logical connective studied in Section 1.1.

(c) $x$ is the author of this book.

---

(a) $\{ x\mid \text{Elizabeth Taylor was once married to }x \}$.
(b) $\{ x\mid x \text{ is a logical connective studied in Section 1.1} \}=\{ \land, \lor, \neg \}$.
(c) $\{ x\mid x \text{ is the author of this book} \}$.
